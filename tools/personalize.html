Make it yours, for Babi ♡

A private, local editor for your anniversary website. No account, no uploads, no backend.

How to use: Customize your writing, then press Choose photo from device inside each memory, star, or letter. The first two memory photos also appear inside the large floating Polaroids. Finally, click Download Personalized ZIP, extract its assets/ folder into your website folder, and merge/replace the files. Everything happens on your device; nothing is uploaded.

🔒 Privacy: This editor runs on your own device. When you publish on GitHub Pages, remove the tools/ folder. Any personal content in a public GitHub Pages repository is publicly accessible, even without a link on the website.
